# Code of Conduct - Anyfy

## 1. Our Goal

At Anyfy, our goal is to build an open, welcoming, inclusive, and respectful community for everyone. We want every contributor and user, regardless of background, identity, or level of experience, to feel safe, valued, and empowered to participate, collaborate, and share ideas.

## 2. Positive Behavior

We encourage practices that strengthen our community culture and support a productive environment. Examples of encouraged behavior include:

- Treating everyone with empathy, respect, and kindness.
- Providing thoughtful, constructive feedback and receiving feedback graciously.
- Practicing patience and understanding with peers.
- Welcoming, guiding, and supporting newcomers to help them get started.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and project as a whole.

## 3. Unacceptable Behavior

To protect our members and foster a healthy collaboration space, certain actions will not be tolerated. Examples include:

- Any form of harassment, intimidation, or bullying.
- Insults, derogatory remarks, and personal or political attacks.
- Sharing private personal data (such as physical addresses, phone numbers, or private emails) without explicit consent.
- Discriminatory language, jokes, or exclusion based on race, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age, religion, or any other characteristic.
- Disruptive trolling or unsolicited commercial promotions.

## 4. Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, or if you have any safety concerns, please reach out directly and confidentially to the project maintainers:

- **Repository Maintainer:** Reach out privately to the repository owner at [https://github.com/devflins2](https://github.com/devflins2) or submit a confidential report via GitHub Private Vulnerability / Maintainer Advisory.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with strict confidentiality. The maintainers are dedicated to safeguarding the privacy and security of anyone submitting a concern.

## 5. Consequences and Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards. When a violation occurs, maintainers will take fair and appropriate action tailored to the context and severity of the incident. Corrective measures may include:

- **Private Warning:** A direct conversation clarifying the violation and expected future behavior.
- **Temporary Ban:** A time-bound suspension from participating in project spaces, discussions, or code contributions.
- **Permanent Ban:** Indefinite removal from all project platforms and communications for severe or repeated infractions.

## 6. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies across all Anyfy project spaces and communication channels. This includes, but is not limited to:

- Project repositories (issues, pull requests, commits, and comments).
- Official discussion forums, chat channels, and mailing lists.
- Any setting or representation where individuals are acting as representatives of the Anyfy project.
